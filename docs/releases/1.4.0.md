### Changes in 1.4.0

- Add **Report plays to Spotify** in Settings, under Playback. When it is on,
  songs you play in Windence can appear in your Spotify history. It is off by
  default. Spotify does not support this for other apps, so turn it on at your
  own risk. The [privacy policy](https://github.com/ForeverInLaw/windence/blob/windows-port/PRIVACY.md)
  describes what is sent.
